# Revision history and archived notes

This page preserves verbatim the revision 5 through 14 release history, retired Vast origin host notes, revision-5 `research.json` packaging notes, and legacy Python/Kover archive notes moved out of [README.md](../README.md).

## Evaluation revisions (5 to 14)

The current source is evaluation revision 14, and the site serves its reports, the [`data-rev14-274-20261112` release](https://github.com/editnori/Genomic-Resistance-Mapping-GRM-/releases/tag/data-rev14-274-20261112). The site served revision 12 until 13 November 2026. Revision 13 was never run on its own, so no revision-13 reports exist. The detailed results above are from revision 14, in that release, apart from the one paragraph marked as revision 7's. The reports of revisions 8 and 9 are in the [`data-rev9-275-20261030` release](https://github.com/editnori/Genomic-Resistance-Mapping-GRM-/releases/tag/data-rev9-275-20261030), and those of revision 12 in the [`data-rev12-274-20261105` release](https://github.com/editnori/Genomic-Resistance-Mapping-GRM-/releases/tag/data-rev12-274-20261105). No release holds revision 7's. Revision 9, with 275 reports over 23 species, was the last release the Vast host served, from 30 October 2026 until that host was destroyed on 31 October 2026 ([deployment.md](deployment.md)). Revision 8 fixes how the lasso stops: a fit that stops at a solver cap can no longer be selected, scored or saved, each report records how every lasso fit stopped, and any unconverged point makes the report invalid ([lasso-stopping.md](lasso-stopping.md)). Two datasets of the revision-8 run stopped on an unconverged lasso fit; revision 9 fixes the active-set solve that stalled there and changes nothing else. Revision 10 adds a species identity check that drops genomes whose sequence does not fit their labelled species ([species-check.md](species-check.md)), and keeps k-mer-identical duplicate genomes whole in one outer test fold, so no genome is scored by a fit that trained on its own copy ([duplicate-isolation.md](duplicate-isolation.md)). Revision 11 keeps those duplicates whole in the random split's inner tuning too, so no tuning partition puts a genome and its copy on both sides. Revision 12 tunes the final models on the same duplicate groups. Revision 13 reads each gene-panel target only for the drugs and organisms its sources name ([src/panels.rs](../src/panels.rs)), so panel rows change and a panel outside its organisms is listed as not scored. Revision 14 scores a null fit's isolates at its fold's training prevalence, so no learner row leaves an isolate unscored, and keeps the revision-13 figure over the fitted folds in `called_metrics` ([equal-scoring.md](equal-scoring.md)). It still writes schema 2 for dataset reports and the report index, which can hold null scores and metrics. The dashboard also reads schema 1, written by revision 6 and earlier, where every learner scored every isolate. Pulling new source recomputes nothing, so each saved report keeps the revision and cohort it was computed with until a run recomputes it.

## Revision 14 release summary notes

The site serves the revision-14 run: 274 reports over 23 species, from 277 curated datasets. Three datasets have no report because one class has fewer than 25 genomes, the class minimum: Escherichia coli amoxicillin, Klebsiella pneumoniae cefotaxime and Staphylococcus aureus methicillin. The published report lists them in `SKIPS.json`.

Revision 14 was run for the same 274 datasets on 12 November 2026, from revision 12's inputs. Of the 274 reports, 162 differ from revision 12 only in the revision number and the run time, 39 in panel rows only, 48 only in the rows of a learner with a null fit, and 25 in both. The best learner changed in 21 datasets. The reports and their release notes are in the [`data-rev14-274-20261112` release](https://github.com/editnori/Genomic-Resistance-Mapping-GRM-/releases/tag/data-rev14-274-20261112). An independent review recomputed the comparison with revision 12 from the files, and a second review checked the release notes. The site has served these reports since 13 November 2026.

[release-summary.md](release-summary.md) gives the main numbers of revisions 12 and 14 side by side: each learner's median balanced accuracy on each split with a species bootstrap interval, the paired drop from the random split to the lineage split, the error-limit counts, and the best learner against the known-gene rule. The scripts that compute them from a release's `index.json` and `intervals/` are in `scripts/release-summary/`, and their outputs for both revisions are in `reference/release-summary/`.

## Revision 7 historical note

The paragraphs above give the numbers of revision 14 and, where they say so, of revision 12. The next one is revision 7's, kept as history: no release holds the revision-7 reports, and the one copy named in this repository, `data/backups/revision7-published-20261025`, was on the laptop only, so its figures cannot be checked from public files. The revision-7 run covered the 13 species that revision 6 reported and recounted every genome. It produced 237 reports for those species' 239 curated datasets. Staphylococcus aureus methicillin and Escherichia coli amoxicillin have no report: among each species' 2,000 selected genomes, methicillin has 24 resistant genomes and amoxicillin has 3 labeled genomes, under the class minimum of 25. Every revision-6 dataset has a revision-7 report, and three datasets are new: Klebsiella pneumoniae doripenem, Mycobacterium tuberculosis levofloxacin, and Salmonella enterica sulfisoxazole. The result checker and metric audit under Checks passed on all 237 reports.

Revision-6 data predates kset digests, and every revision-6 report is recomputed because the evaluation revision changed, so one pipeline run recounts, rebuilds the stages that no longer match, and relearns. Name the species with `--species`; without it, the pipeline also downloads and counts every curated species that has no counts yet. Until the run finishes, the dashboard shows the new revision's reports for finished species beside revision-6 reports for the rest.

This is internal validation: cross-validation on public BV-BRC genomes scored against their reported calls. No external, prospective, or clinical validation has been done.

## Retired origin host (Vast + Cloudflare Tunnel)

Until 31 October 2026 one `grm serve` on a Vast container served the site behind a Cloudflare Tunnel. That host was destroyed that day. [deployment.md](deployment.md) covers that host's toolkit, which stays in the repository in case the site needs a host again: local preview of a released report tree, publishing a report copy, building and staging a release, activation, rollback, restoring after a container restart, and the tunnel connector. The publish, build, stage, activate, rollback, restore and legacy-approval scripts in `scripts/deployment/` change nothing under the deployment root without `--apply`; `connector.sh run` and `stop` act at once, and `status.sh` and `smoke.sh` write only a temporary directory. Before serving a pair, the scripts verify the build and report trees against their SHA-256 manifests and require a stage approval bound to both manifests (`status.sh` does not verify manifests). Approvals and state files are strict one-record ASCII files, written atomically and read back. Every server start is journaled, and every process it starts holds a locked spawn marker from the fork on, so an interrupted or killed start is found and stopped, and its journal is kept while the lock shows a survivor the scripts cannot see. A rollback identifies a damaged current server by its recorded start (PID, start time, token and marker), not by its executable. Records, locks and logs are opened only when they are regular files, so a link or FIFO is refused without blocking. The tunnel token is read only by cloudflared, through `--token-file`.

## Revision-5 `research.json` packaging

`research.json` is an optional dashboard file with two revision-5 analyses: GRM's MIC model beside a baseline that predicts the MIC without the genome, and selective calling, which finds the datasets where the genome alone can make the R/S call for a confident subset of isolates. `grm research` packages their saved outputs from `data/research/` without rerunning the analyses:

```bash
./target/release/grm research \
  --revision5-reports /path/to/revision5/datasets \
  --out data/report/research.json
```

It verifies the revision-5 reports against the snapshot hash list pinned in the source and records each input's hash. Each entry carries SHA-256 digests of the sample ids, labels, lineages, MICs, and held-out predictions its analysis read, and the dashboard shows its numbers only when the report on screen gives the same digests, because a later revision can keep a dataset id and change its genomes. Mismatched entries, missing entries, reports without an MIC model, and entries withheld because their MIC model read disk-diffusion zone diameters as MICs each show as their own state. Without `research.json`, the dashboard works as usual.

The saved inputs are not published, so the command cannot be run from public files. It needs, under `<data>/research/`, the hash list `RESEARCH-SNAPSHOT-SHA256SUMS` with the sha256 pinned in `src/research.rs`; the MIC lane's folder `grm-research-mic-first-r1/` (`receipt.md`, `out/analysis/prediction_quality.csv`, `out/pairs/pairs.tsv.gz`, and `out/splits_final/<id>.json` for each dataset it refitted); the selective-calling lane's folder `grm-research-selective-calling-r1/` (`receipt.md` and `out/species_drug_summary.csv`); and the revision-5 dataset reports that hash list names. No release holds the revision-5 reports, and the report trees of the revision 9 and 14 releases hold no `research.json`. The site answers 404 for `/data/research.json` ([static-hosting.md](static-hosting.md)), so it shows no revision-5 research.

## Legacy Python/Kover archive

The original Python/Kover application and its saved results are in the [legacy archive](https://github.com/editnori/Genomic-Resistance-Mapping-GRM-/releases/tag/legacy-archive). `grm report` reads 73 historical Kover rows from `reference/kover-summary.json`, relative to the working folder (`--kover` takes another path), and refuses if that file is missing or doesn't parse. `grm pipeline` has no `--kover`: it reads the file only from that path, so run it from the source folder, and it checks the file before counting. The dashboard shows the one for the same species and antibiotic as historical context, because Kover used other cohorts and evaluation procedures.
