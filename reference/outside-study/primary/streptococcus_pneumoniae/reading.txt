Outside-study test, streptococcus_pneumoniae, primary population
This is a retrospective test on newly deposited studies from the same database.
It is not a lockbox: the batch's metadata and label totals were read before the rules were frozen.
The genomes come from the same database as the cohort.

Frozen genomes: 503. Scored: 503 (3 of them borderline in the species check, kept and flagged).
Genomes excluded, no sequence: 0.
Genomes excluded, species check failed: 0.
Genomes excluded, not counted: 0.

Each model is the report's final model, fitted once on the whole cohort and applied unchanged.
The report's figures are for models refitted in each cross-validation fold and scored on held-out cohort genomes.
Calls use the 0.5 cutoff. Intervals are exact Clopper-Pearson 95% intervals.
A dataset with fewer than 25 resistant or 25 susceptible scored genomes is reported as counts only.
Sensitivity and specificity are over the genomes a learner called; genomes with no call are counted in their own column.
The known-gene rule's reference figure is a fixed rule applied to the whole cohort: nothing was fitted, so there is no cross-validation figure for it.
The datasets share genomes and studies, so they are not independent of each other.
Precision depends on the share of resistant genomes, which differs between the cohort and the new genomes.
Scored genomes with the same k-mer set as a cohort genome: 0; as another scored genome: 2 (listed in duplicates.tsv; none is excluded for it).
Stage attempts: setup 1, fetch 1, species-check 1, count 1, known-gene 1, predict 1.
