## B1. The primary reading

The plan's rule stays the primary reading, unchanged (PLAN.md sections 6 and 8, as ADDENDUM A3 and A4 run them): estimable at 25 R and 25 S; recall with a Clopper-Pearson 95% interval; log loss with p clipped to [1e-4, 1 - 1e-4] and a 95% interval from 2,000 genome resamples, seed 1; each read against the lineage split's figure; a dataset holds when recall is not below and log loss is not worse; a learner carries over in a species when it holds on more than half of at least 3 estimable datasets, does not carry over when it holds on half or fewer, and is not assessable with fewer than 3. The answer and the per-learner counts come from this reading alone.

## B2. Three more readings, beside the primary one

For each species and learner, three more readings, defined as the review computes them in `revoutside/scripts/recompute_metrics.py` (sha256 `d6c87a15ee4c09f214f930f770a6ffac285ce9254ccf74240f122fe19d12e0b8`) and `revoutside/scripts/exploratory_readings.py` (sha256 `cbbfe6423229d021a9f2435bc27e75548198cd548db85f8c8d62289c97e32b3c`). Seed 1 in every reading, 2,000 resamples, Python `random.Random(1).choices`, nearest-rank percentiles (the 50th and the 1,950th of 2,000 sorted values).

- L. The lineage figure with its own interval (recompute_metrics.py, its point 4.3). Lineage recall interval: 1 minus the upper and lower bounds of the very major error rate in the release's `intervals/<dataset>.json` for the lineage split and the learner; where there is no such entry, Clopper-Pearson on the released lineage scores, a score of 0.5 or more counted as a resistant call. Lineage log loss interval: 2,000 resamples, seed 1, of the released per-genome lineage losses, same clip. The pilot's intervals are those of B1. Recall is below when the pilot's upper bound is under the lineage lower bound; log loss is worse when the pilot's lower bound is over the lineage upper bound. A dataset holds when neither.
- C. Class-balanced log loss on both sides (exploratory_readings.py, reading C): the mean of the resistant genomes' mean loss and the susceptible genomes' mean loss. Pilot interval from a bootstrap stratified by class (each resample draws the resistant genomes, then the susceptible genomes, from one `random.Random(1)`); the lineage side is the fixed balanced figure from the released scores. Worse when the pilot's lower bound is over the lineage figure. A dataset holds when B1's recall reading is not below and C is not worse.
- D. BioProject cluster bootstrap on the pilot side (exploratory_readings.py, reading D): the scored, labelled genomes grouped by the BioProject field of `selected.tsv` (the field's text as one key; a genome with none is its own group); 2,000 resamples of groups with replacement, `random.Random(1)`; recall and log loss recomputed on each resample, resamples with no resistant genome left out of the recall interval; the lineage figure fixed. A dataset holds when recall is not below and log loss is not worse.

Each reading gives its own holds count for each species and learner, and its own result under the plan's pass rule. A difference between two learners (in the pass rule's result for a species, or in the count of species where each carries over) is stated only where it holds in all four readings, B1, L, C and D. Otherwise the report writes that the data do not separate them.
